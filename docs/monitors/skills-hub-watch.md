# Skills Hub Watch

*Runbook of the Skills Hub Watch. Repo-wide rules live in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md); the other monitors are listed in the [index](README.md).*

Watches the Microsoft Skills Hub blog (Tech Community), the channel where Microsoft announces new, renamed and retiring exams and certifications, beta windows, vouchers and credential program changes, often weeks before the Microsoft Learn pages change. The hub's beta rule needs the official announcement (`AGENTS.md > Beta Exam Pages`: the beta start date, and the expected go-live month when Microsoft names one, come from the announcement post), and until now nothing watched it. Not rendered on the site, not part of the build.

- **Workflow:** `.github/workflows/skills-hub-watch.yml` — every 6 hours at minute 20 (00:20, 06:20, 12:20 and 18:20 UTC; no other workflow that pushes to `main` starts at the same time, other monitors start at minute 20 too, at other hours, and `tests/scripts/workflows.test.mjs` keeps the whole set free of start-time collisions, which is only a convenience because scheduled runs often start hours late: the three-try push retry of the commit step is the protection) + manual trigger with a `force_baseline` checkbox. A run is never cancelled half way (a newer run waits).
- **Script:** `scripts/skills-hub-watch.mjs` — Node, no dependencies (it imports nothing but `node:` modules and `scripts/lib/`; it does not import the New Exam Monitor's script); run with `node scripts/skills-hub-watch.mjs`. Tests, fully offline (a trimmed copy of the real feed in `tests/fixtures/skills-hub/feed.xml`, a feed whose article mentions a voucher in `feed-voucher-in-article.xml`, a synthetic announcement post of the beta exam AZ-401 in `feed-beta-announcement.xml`, a local fake of the feed, and the workflow's schedule, step order and permissions), in `tests/scripts/skills-hub-watch.test.mjs`.
- **Tracker:** `src/data_files/skills-hub-posts.json` — `{ lastSynced, problem, posts: [{ id, title, link?, pubDate, matched }] }`, the newest 200 posts the monitor has seen (`matched` is the relevance verdict at the time). It is rewritten only when a new post is recorded or the `problem` counter changes (`lastSynced` moves only with new posts), so a quiet day, a stale feed snapshot or a long outage produces no commit. A post is identified by its numeric Tech Community id (`/ba-p/<id>`), so an edited title or slug is never a new post.
- **Source:** `https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/t5/s/gxcuf89792/rss/board?board.id=skills-hub-blog`, the RSS feed the blog page itself links (its RSS link `https://aka.ms/MTC/RSS-Board-Footer?board.id=skills-hub-blog` redirects to it). Verified 2026-10-14: HTTP 200, `text/xml`, the 20 newest posts with their full text, no categories, about 240 KB. RSS 2.0, RSS 1.0 and Atom are understood. No other feed is linked from the blog page; do not guess feed URLs, add one only when a Microsoft page links it.
- **Relevance filter:** a post is relevant when the regular expression `/exam|certif|retir|beta|voucher|renew|roundup|credential|applied skill|badge|discount/i` (the constant `RELEVANCE_RE`, the only place it is defined) matches its title, its summary or its categories. The summary is the first 600 characters of the text (the teaser): the feed holds the whole article, and matching all of it matched 19 of the 20 posts. The terms are plain substrings on purpose (`exam` also matches "example"): a post reported for nothing costs a glance, a missed announcement costs an outdated page. A second, much stricter pass reads the WHOLE article for the few words that cannot be incidental in a post of this blog (`BODY_RELEVANCE` in the script): `voucher`, `retire`/`retires`/`retired`/`retiring`/`retirement`, `beta exam`, and an exam code like `AB-650` (also with a non-breaking hyphen). Two real posts of 2026-06 announced free exam vouchers in their body under a title and a teaser about the AI Skills Fest ("Free Microsoft Certification exam vouchers", "receive a voucher for select Certification exams") and the broad filter missed both; on the 20 live posts of 2026-10-14 the stricter pass adds exactly those 2 and no other (the broad words stay on the title and the teaser because they appear in almost every article). A post that matched only through its article says so in the issue ("Matched on `voucher` in the article text only: the title and the teaser do not mention it, so read the whole post"), and a broad match lists what the article adds ("also in the article text"); the checklist items follow the stricter terms too. On 2026-10-14, 12 of the 20 live posts were relevant (10 by the broad filter). Every new post is recorded, relevant or not; only the relevant ones are reported.
- **On a new relevant post:** ONE GitHub issue per run (`enhancement`, no assignee) titled `Skills Hub post: <title>` or `Skills Hub posts (3): a; b +1 more` (at most 200 characters). Per post: title and link (only `microsoft.com` and `aka.ms` hosts are linked), publication date, the filter terms that matched, the exam codes in the post, a 300-character neutral excerpt in a quote block, and the sentences that state a date (verbatim, in a code block, labelled as not verified), then a "What to do" checklist that points at this runbook. Exam codes are `[A-Z]{2,3}-\d{3}` (also with a non-breaking hyphen) and are split into: hub page exists (with its `exam-status.mjs` status and any voucher page whose `linkedExam` is that code), no hub page but already on Learn (`learn-exams.json`, the [New Exam Monitor](new-exam-monitor.md) tracks it), no hub page and not on Learn yet (the post is ahead of Learn), and not a hub exam family. Anything that cannot be read (for example `learn-exams.json` before the [New Exam Monitor](new-exam-monitor.md) wrote it) is said in the issue and never changes whether a post is reported. A post that names the code of a tracked beta exam (an entry of `beta-exams.json` that is not `flaggedGA`) whose `announcement` is still `pending` or `unannounced` is the announcement that exam's beta note is waiting for: its entry in the issue starts with an **ACTION** block with the exact steps (record the post's address as `announcement`, regenerate the note with `scripts/beta-note.mjs`, add the beta voucher page, re-run the tests). An exam whose announcement already is an address, and a post that names no such exam, change nothing; a `beta-exams.json` that cannot be read is said so in the issue and never fails the run (`BETA_EXAMS_FILE`). The report shrinks in steps to stay under 60,000 characters.
- **First run** (no tracker, or `force_baseline`): a silent baseline, no issue. The newest 5 relevant posts go into the job summary so the maintainers can see what was swallowed. Trigger the workflow manually once after it is on `main`; it commits the baseline itself, like the other monitors.
- **Untrusted text:** titles, excerpts and cells are plain text without HTML, links, `@mentions`, `#123` or `GH-123` references, code spans, table breaks or emphasis syntax (a zero-width space defuses the references); quoted sentences sit in code fences with the fence characters neutralised.

## Failsafes
- **A failed fetch is never evidence.** The tracker keeps its posts and a counter (`problem`) counts consecutive runs without a usable feed. Definite answers (HTTP 200 that is not an RSS or Atom document, fewer than `MIN_FEED_ITEMS` (5) usable items, a response over `MAX_FEED_BYTES`, 404, 410) raise the alert on the `DEFINITE_FAILURE_RUNS`-th (2) consecutive run; a feed that merely cannot be fetched (5xx after the retries, 429, timeouts, network errors, 401/403) after `FETCH_FAILURE_RUNS` (8) runs, about two days. The alert is ONE `bug` issue with the stable title `Skills Hub Watch Failed` (a comment for every further alert, at most one a day: `--repeat-hours 24` of `scripts/failure-issue.mjs`, because a failsafe that stays tripped fails the run every 6 hours, which used to open 4 issues a day) and the run ends red; the alert repeats every `REMINDER_DAYS` (7) while a feed problem lasts, the red run on every run. The counter stops growing once the problem is established, and the next good read clears it. The workflow closes the issue with a comment only on a run that read a usable feed and saw nothing fail (`extraction_failed` is `false` and `feed_read` is `true`): a first or second bad read sets none of `extraction_failed`, `feed_problem` and `feed_read`, so it is no evidence of a problem and none of health either, and it must not close an issue that the failsafe below or a corrupt tracker raised; a feed that is still unreadable keeps the issue open on the days the script does not repeat the alert.
- **Too many new posts at once:** more than `MAX_NEW_POSTS` (10) new posts in one read is the feed changing shape (another board, another id scheme), not news. Nothing is written and a `bug` issue is raised. If the posts are real, run the workflow with `force_baseline` ticked: every post is then recorded silently, so read the latest posts of the blog yourself.
- **Stale snapshots:** the CDN in front of the feed serves different snapshots within seconds of each other (verified 2026-10-14: three snapshots in a row), and an older snapshot holds posts that already rolled out of a newer one's window of 20. An unseen post more than `OLD_POST_DAYS` (30) older than the newest known post is such an artefact: it is neither reported nor recorded (the job summary counts them); a really new post is never older than the newest one. `OLD_POST_DAYS=0` switches it off.
- **Order:** the issue is opened BEFORE the tracker is committed, like the [New Exam Monitor](new-exam-monitor.md): when the issue step fails the commit is skipped and the next run (or "Re-run failed jobs") reports the same posts again; committing first would lose the alert. The price is a possible duplicate issue when the commit fails after the issue was opened (the commit retries its push three times).
- **Known limits:** the monitor sees the 20 newest posts, so it would only lose a post if 20 were published within 6 hours; a post that matches none of the filter terms in its title or teaser is recorded but not reported; an announcement that is only on a Learn page is the [New Exam Monitor](new-exam-monitor.md)'s job; a post edited after it was reported is not reported again.

## Env
`FEED_URL`, `TRACKER_FILE`, `DOCS_DIR`, `EXAM_STATUS_FILE`, `LEARN_TRACKER_FILE`, `BETA_EXAMS_FILE`, `REPORT_FILE`, `ERROR_FILE`, `GITHUB_OUTPUT`, `GITHUB_STEP_SUMMARY`, `FETCH_ATTEMPTS` (3), `RETRY_BASE_MS`, `MAX_RETRY_AFTER_MS`, `FETCH_TIMEOUT_MS`, `MAX_FEED_BYTES`, `MIN_FEED_ITEMS`, `MAX_NEW_POSTS`, `MAX_TRACKED_POSTS`, `OLD_POST_DAYS`, `DEFINITE_FAILURE_RUNS`, `FETCH_FAILURE_RUNS`, `REMINDER_DAYS`, `FORCE_BASELINE`, `TODAY`; the script header lists the defaults. To try it without touching the committed tracker: `TRACKER_FILE=/tmp/skills-hub-posts.json REPORT_FILE=/tmp/report.md ERROR_FILE=/tmp/error.md GITHUB_OUTPUT=/tmp/out node scripts/skills-hub-watch.mjs` (run it twice for the no-op check). It writes the `GITHUB_OUTPUT` keys `changes_found`, `baseline_updated`, `extraction_failed`, `feed_problem`, `feed_read` (a usable feed was read and nothing failed: the only run that may close the failure issue), `issue_title`, `posts_new` and `posts_relevant`.

## Working the list
1. Open each post in full. Nothing in the issue is verified: confirm every exam name, code, date and URL on Microsoft Learn before it goes into the hub (`AGENTS.md > Content Rules`).
2. New or renamed exam or certification: create the page as under [New Exam Monitor](new-exam-monitor.md) > "Working the list" (`node scripts/new-exam.mjs --code <CODE> --name "<official name>"`, and for a beta exam `--beta --beta-start YYYY-MM-DD` with the day the beta period began, see step 3), or check the existing exam page's title, description and resources against the post. This post usually comes weeks before the [New Exam Monitor](new-exam-monitor.md)'s issue for the same exam.
3. Beta exam: this post is the official announcement that `AGENTS.md > Beta Exam Pages` asks for. The beta start date for the `:::note` timeline is the date the post gives for the beta period (or the first day the exam could be scheduled), not the day the post was published, unless the post says so; if it gives none, keep the `betaStart` of the entry, and if the entry has none either, ask for it instead of publishing a date. Set `betaStart` and `announcement` in its `beta-exams.json` entry: `announcement` becomes the URL of the post (the post puts the exam in the `announced` state) and the note is rewritten as `announced` with `node scripts/beta-note.mjs --code <CODE>`. When the entry waits for exactly this announcement (`pending` or `unannounced`), the issue's entry for the post starts with an **ACTION** block that lists these steps. If the post gives an expected go-live date or month (the dated sentences in the issue may show it), the note states it exactly as Microsoft wrote it (`--expects "<its wording>"`), linked to the post, next to the calculated 10-12 week window and not instead of it, never more precise than the post; if it gives none, the note only links the post (`AGENTS.md > Beta Exam Pages`, "Announcement states" and Rules). An exam without the `BETA` status and the `beta-exams.json` entry gets them first ([Adding a new exam to either tracker](beta-and-retiring-exams.md#adding-a-new-exam-to-either-tracker)).
4. Voucher: add or update the voucher page (`AGENTS.md > Voucher Pages`; for a beta discount write the page with `node scripts/new-beta-voucher.mjs`, which follows the one beta voucher layout), with `voucherEnds` and `linkedExam` from the post, and point its "Read Official Announcement" button at the post.
5. Retirement or transition: follow the retirement rules (`:::caution` banner with the verified replacement, `RETIRING` badge, `retiring-exams.json`); a possible but unconfirmed change gets the `AGENTS.md > At Risk Exam Treatment`.
6. A post that is not about an exam, certification or voucher the hub covers needs no edit. Nothing needs to be dismissed: every post is recorded and is not reported again.
