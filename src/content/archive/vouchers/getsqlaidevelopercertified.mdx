title: Get SQL AI Developer Certified (Expired Offer)
description: Historical June 2026 Microsoft Partner offer for a 100% DP-800 exam voucher.
---

Microsoft Partner employees could request a 100% voucher for **DP-800: Developing AI-Enabled Database Solutions**. The offer ended **June 30, 2026**. Vouchers were distributed by private message in the Fabric Community, not by email.

The former process required joining the Fabric Community and SQL Partner Community, then submitting the [campaign form](https://community.fabric.microsoft.com/t5/custom/page/page-id/campaign_form?campaignID=Y2FtcGFpZ24tMTc3ODcwMjA1Njk4Mw==&WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290).
