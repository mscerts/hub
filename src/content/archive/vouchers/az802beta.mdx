title: Exam AZ-802 Beta Voucher (Expired Offer)
description: Historical 80% beta exam discount offer for AZ-802, available through July 22, 2026.

The former public offer gave the first 300 candidates who took **Exam AZ-802 (beta)** on or before **July 22, 2026** an 80% discount with code `AZ802Chandler`. The offer is no longer active.

AZ-802 replaced AZ-800 and AZ-801, consolidating both exams into one. See the [Microsoft announcement](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/skills-hub-blog/new-exam-for-microsoft-certified-windows-server-administrator-associate-certific/4494120?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290).
