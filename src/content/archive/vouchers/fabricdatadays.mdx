title: Fabric Data Days (Expired Offer)
description: Historical 2026 Fabric Data Days offer for free DP-600, DP-700, or DP-800 exam vouchers.
---

This offer provided a 100% exam voucher for DP-600, DP-700, or DP-800, while supplies lasted. It was limited to 20,000 vouchers.

## Former eligibility steps

1. Join the Fabric Community.
2. Watch two live or on-demand Data Days sessions.
3. Complete three Microsoft Learn modules from the campaign collection.
4. Accept the campaign terms and submit the voucher request by **August 10, 2026**.
5. Check Fabric Community private messages after approximately two weeks for eligibility.

The offer is no longer active. Its former links: [Data Days schedule](https://aka.ms/dd/schedule?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290), [learning collection](https://aka.ms/dd/modules?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290), and [Fabric Community](https://community.fabric.microsoft.com/?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290).
