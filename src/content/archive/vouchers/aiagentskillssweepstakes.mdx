---
title: AI Agent Skills Sweepstakes (Expired Offer)
description: Historical June–July 2026 sweepstakes offering a chance to win a 100% Microsoft exam voucher.
---

The sweepstakes ran from **June 15 to July 15, 2026**. Eligible participants earned an eligible Microsoft Applied Skills credential and submitted the official entry form for a chance to win one of 900 full exam vouchers for AB-100, AB-900, or AB-620. Winners were to be drawn July 16, with voucher redemption through November 30, 2026.

The offer is no longer active. Historical references: [official rules](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/microsoft-ai-agent-skills-sweepstakes-official-rules?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290), [entry form](https://aka.ms/AIEntryForm), and [Microsoft announcement](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/microsoft-ai-agent-skills-sweepstakes?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290).
