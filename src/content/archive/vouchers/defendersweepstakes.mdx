---
title: Microsoft Defender Challenge Sweepstakes (Expired Offer)
description: Historical July–August 2026 sweepstakes for a chance to win a 50% SC-200 or SC-500 exam voucher.
---

The sweepstakes ran from **July 20 to August 21, 2026**. Participants completed the Microsoft Defender Skilling Challenge and submitted an entry for a chance to win one of 500 vouchers worth 50% off SC-200 or SC-500. Winners were selected during August 24–31, 2026.

The offer is no longer active. Historical references: [official rules](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/microsoft-defender-challenge-sweepstakes-terms-and-conditions?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290) and [Defender Skilling Challenge](https://learn.microsoft.com/challenges/6381s7tkn541nz?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290).
