## Retirement

Microsoft retired Exam AZ-204 on July 31, 2026. The hub's former study page pointed candidates to [AI-200: Developing AI Cloud Solutions on Azure](/certs/azure/ai-200/) as its successor.

The Azure Developer Associate certification remains listed as a prerequisite option for DevOps Engineer Expert. The retired AZ-204 exam cannot be taken now.
