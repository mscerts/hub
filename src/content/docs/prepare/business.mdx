Business exams are introductory, but they can still test detailed distinctions between products, capabilities, roles, and business scenarios. Previous technical experience is not normally the focus; familiarity with the product and the decisions made by its users is more valuable.

## Study Time

As a starting point, allow about one week of focused preparation, then adjust based on your familiarity with the product and your results on unfamiliar practice questions. Use objective coverage and understanding, not elapsed time, to decide when you are ready.

## How to Prepare

### How Current Business Exams Differ

The current business exams have different experience expectations:

- **AB-730** expects familiarity with generative AI productivity tools, including Microsoft 365 Copilot and agents, and comfort using core Microsoft 365 apps for common business tasks. Hands-on product use is an important part of preparation.
- **AB-731** focuses on recognizing AI opportunities, planning adoption, aligning investments with business goals, and leading organizational change. It does not require coding, but Microsoft expects familiarity with Microsoft 365 services, Microsoft Foundry, general AI capabilities, and business adoption or change-management work.
- **GH-700** targets product managers, program managers, business analysts, and other non-coding business roles using GitHub and GitHub Copilot to design, automate, and govern agentic business workflows with human-in-the-loop oversight.

Use the current study guide for the exact scope (and check the exam page's **Study Guide Changes** card for upcoming updates) because Microsoft can revise these exams and introduce additional business credentials.

### Using MSFTHub Study Guide Courses

Every current Business exam page on the Hub includes a **Study Guide Course** in its **Text** tab that follows this study-guide preparation approach directly. Each skill group opens with **Fundamentals** for core concepts, and every study-guide task links the matching Microsoft Learn training modules and product documentation, including for newer or beta business exams that do not have a standalone official Learn course yet and with dual current/upcoming views when an exam's study guide is scheduled to change.

<Steps>
1. Read the study guide, check the exam page's **Study Guide Changes** card, and turn each objective into a checklist item.
2. Work task by task through the Hub's **Study Guide Course** (in your exam page's **Text** tab), or complete the official Microsoft Learn course and matching product documentation so every objective is covered.
3. Practice applicable product tasks when the objectives describe using or configuring an end-user tool. See [Hands-on Exam Practice](/certs/prepare/labs/) for environment guidance.
4. Take the official Practice Assessment if one is available for your exam. Research every incorrect or uncertain answer instead of memorizing the question.
5. Use [Navigating Microsoft Certification Study Materials](/certs/prepare/studymaterials/) and open your exam on [Microsoft Exam Study Materials](/certs/) only when you need another explanation or more practice.
6. Try the [Microsoft Certification Exam Sandbox](https://aka.ms/examdemo) and read the [Exam Experience](/certs/guide/takingtheexams/) guide before exam day.
</Steps>

## How to Know When You Are Ready

You should be able to explain each objective in your own words, distinguish between similar products or capabilities, and choose an appropriate solution for a business scenario. Practice Assessment results are most useful when the questions are still unfamiliar and you can explain why each alternative is right or wrong.
