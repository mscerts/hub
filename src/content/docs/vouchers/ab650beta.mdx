Exam AB-650 is the new beta exam for Microsoft 365 Certified: AI Services Administrator Associate. It validates your ability to configure, manage, secure, and govern Microsoft 365 tenants, workloads, and AI services.

## How to claim the 80% discount

<Steps>

1. Register for [Exam AB-650 (beta)](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/ai-services-administrator-associate/?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290).
2. Schedule to take the exam on or before **August 18, 2026**.
3. When prompted for payment, use the code **AB-650SkyClub** to receive the 80% discount.

</Steps>

:::tip
This is not a private access code. The seats are offered on a first-come, first-served basis to the first 300 people. This discount is not available in Turkey, Pakistan, India, or China.
