:::danger
Microsoft has a history of retroactively changing terms and conditions of their voucher offers. Everyone is encouraged to complete this opportunity as soon as possible and redeem their voucher as soon as possible to avoid potentially losing your voucher due to Microsoft changing their mind.
:::

:::note
LevelUp runs two Certification Week voucher offers at the same time. In both, you earn a 100% off exam voucher by passing the practice test of a free LevelUp certification prep course, and you get one voucher per learner per offer. None of the vouchers can be applied to Microsoft Certified: Fundamentals exams.
:::

:::caution
Both offers are only available to employees of Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program (MAICPP) partners. The email address registered on LevelUp must belong to an active partner domain, based on the partner list Microsoft approved as of July 31, 2026, and each partner domain has a maximum number of vouchers. Vouchers go out first-come, first-served until stocks last, and issuing one is subject to availability and Microsoft approval. Microsoft employees and Microsoft vendors with a v-dash account are not eligible.
:::

## How to claim a voucher

The steps are the same for both offers.

<Steps>

1. Make sure the email address you use belongs to a Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program (MAICPP) partner organization. LevelUp only issues vouchers to learners whose registered email is on an active partner domain. The [Microsoft Partner Employees](/certs/guide/partneremployees/) page shows how to check whether your company is a partner.
2. Create or log in to your [LevelUp account](https://skillupwithlevelup.com/register) with that email address.
3. Open the [Certification Prep On-Demand catalog](https://skillupwithlevelup.com/certification-prep-on-demand?voucher_offer=yes#cert-course-catalog) and select **Enroll Now** on one of the eligible courses.
4. Complete the required module (Welcome to cert prep) and pass the course's practice test with a score of 80% or higher. You only get one attempt per offer, and a practice test passed before the offer's start date does not count.
5. If you qualify, LevelUp emails your voucher code within 5-7 business days of passing. Check your spam/junk folder.
6. Redeem the voucher within 60 days of issuance, following Pearson VUE's terms as applied through [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/training/?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290) when you schedule your exam. If redeeming fails (for example with an "Invalid Voucher" error), submit a request through the LevelUp Helpdesk.

</Steps>
