## How to claim the voucher

<Steps>

1. Create or log in to your [LevelUp account](https://skillupwithlevelup.com/register).
2. Browse the [Certification Prep On-Demand catalog](https://skillupwithlevelup.com/certification-prep-on-demand?voucher_offer=yes#cert-course-catalog) and enroll in AB-100, AI-103, or GH-300.
3. Complete the course and score 80% or higher on its practice test. You only get one attempt.
4. If you qualify, LevelUp emails your voucher code within 5–7 business days of passing. Check your spam/junk folder.
5. Redeem the voucher within 60 days of issuance, following Pearson VUE's terms as applied through [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/training/?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290) when you schedule your exam.

</Steps>
