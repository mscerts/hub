GitHub Learn at GitHub Universe 2026 Preview is a free Microsoft Reactor livestream on **October 21, 2026, 6-7 PM UTC**, hosted by Ari LiVigni (Senior Learning Advocate at GitHub). The session covers the GitHub Copilot Proficiency Model and Insiders List, and gives a first look at **GH-700 (beta): Enhancing Operational Efficiency with Agentic AI**, a new exam leading to the GitHub Certified: Agentic AI Business Professional certification, built for product managers, program managers, technical project managers, and business analysts. See the [GH-700 study materials](/certs/github/gh-700/) page for the official resources of the exam itself.

## How to get the voucher

<Steps>

1. [Register for the event](https://developer.microsoft.com/en-us/reactor/events/27591/) with your email (or sign in with a Microsoft Account).
2. Attend the livestream on October 21, 2026, 6-7 PM UTC.
3. Stay tuned until the end of the live broadcast to claim the exclusive exam voucher discount code for your next certification.
