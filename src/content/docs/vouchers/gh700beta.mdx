Exam GH-700: Enhancing Operational Efficiency with Agentic AI is the new beta exam for GitHub Certified: Agentic AI Business Professional. It validates your skills in using GitHub and GitHub Copilot to design agentic workflows and apply human oversight. The certification is designed for business professionals such as product managers, program managers, technical project managers, and business analysts, and deep software development or coding experience is not required.
