Data Days: AI @ Work runs from **November 2 to December 15, 2026**. Attendees who meet the requirements can request one exam voucher: **100% off** for DP-600, DP-700 or DP-800, or **50% off** for PL-300, DP-900, DP-420 or DP-300. ## The offers

Pick the offer that matches your exam and when you can take it.

| Discount | Exams                          | Take your exam by |
| -------- | ------------------------------ | ----------------- |
| 100% off | DP-600, DP-700, DP-800         | November 30, 2026 |
| 100% off | DP-600, DP-700, DP-800         | December 31, 2026 |
| 50% off  | PL-300, DP-900, DP-420, DP-300 | January 31, 2027  |

:::tip
Voucher quantities are limited, so you have a bigger chance of getting a voucher if you request the first offer (take your exam by November 30, 2026). Microsoft does not say how vouchers are split between the offers, so this is our advice and not a guarantee.
:::

A voucher works for one exam, and only for the exams listed for its offer. The 100% offers share one request form, where you agree to the date that fits you.

## How to claim a voucher

:::note
The two Data Days sessions you need to watch to qualify do not start until Tuesday, November 3, 2026. The first one is "Microsoft Fabric at Scale: Build, Deploy, and Govern" at 8:00 AM PT. See the [full schedule](#data-days-schedule) below.
:::

<Steps>

1. Pick your offer: 100% for DP-600, DP-700 or DP-800, or 50% for PL-300, DP-900, DP-420 or DP-300.
2. Read the terms and conditions of your offer: the [100% exam voucher terms](https://aka.ms/dd/cert100/terms) or the [50% exam voucher terms](https://aka.ms/dd/cert50/terms). They list the requirements, the rules and the countries where the offer is not valid.
3. Join the free [Microsoft Fabric Community](https://community.fabric.microsoft.com/) if you are not a member yet. You need to sign in to fill out the form.
4. Watch two Data Days sessions, live or on-demand. Pick them from the [Data Days schedule](#data-days-schedule); the first sessions start on November 3, 2026.
5. For the 100% offer, score 85% or better on the practice assessment of the exam you want: DP-600, DP-700 or DP-800.
6. Fill out the request form for your offer, by December 10, 2026, and agree to take your exam by the date of your offer.
7. Watch your Fabric Community inbox. If you are eligible, your voucher code arrives by private message on the Fabric Community within about a week. It is not sent by email.
8. Schedule your exam right away with Pearson VUE, at a test center or online. The private message includes the date you must use the voucher by and the date you must take the exam by.

</Steps>

:::caution
Good to know before you request:

- You can request one voucher per person, and vouchers cannot be extended: schedule your exam as soon as you get your code.
- You must take your exam by the date in your voucher message. A voucher that is not used by its expiration date is reclaimed.
- You may forfeit the voucher if you miss your exam appointment, or cancel or reschedule it 72 hours or less before it.
- The voucher is not transferable, cannot be sold, exchanged or refunded, and can only be redeemed once.
- You must be older than 14. Government employees should check with their employer before redeeming.
- The offer is not valid in Belarus, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Russia, Syria, the Region of Crimea and where prohibited. The 50% offer is also not valid in Bangladesh, Benin, Cameroon, Ghana, Lebanon, Nigeria, Pakistan, Senegal, Tanzania, Togo and Uganda.
- The offer is subject to availability and can be ended at any time.

:::

:::note
The 50% terms page says to take the exam by January 31, 2026, while the offer page says January 31, 2027. This page follows the offer page, and the date in your voucher message is the one that counts.
:::
