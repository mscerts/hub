## Certification courses

16 courses across three tracks are included. The voucher you earn should be a general role-based certification exam voucher. It's good for any Microsoft role-based certification exam, not just the course you attended.

- **Security**: SC-200, SC-401, SC-500
- **Cloud + AI**: AZ-305, AI-200, DP-600, GH-600
- **AI Business Solutions**: AB-100, AB-410, AB-210, AB-250, MD-102, MS-700, MS-721, PL-400 (plus AB-620 or MB-800, depending on region)

<Steps>
1. Register for your regional Certification Week using your work email (public domains like Gmail or Outlook are rejected) to confirm your Microsoft partner status.
2. Wait for registration confirmation. Approved registrants receive an invite within 5–7 business days.
3. Attend the live, instructor-led sessions during the event week (every day).
4. Complete the self-study lab exercises for each certification (about 1 hour per day, per certification).
5. Take the LevelUp eligibility assessment and score 80% or higher on your first attempt to qualify for one free Microsoft exam voucher per event.
6. Redeem your voucher and schedule your exam.
    - [Terms and conditions apply.](https://skillupwithlevelup.com/cert-terms-and-conditions)
</Steps>

## Sales Certification Weeks

The Microsoft Sales Certification Weeks are a separate, shorter format for partner sellers, sales leaders, business development professionals, and customer-facing sales roles. They run as three half-day sessions and cover exam readiness for AB-730 (AI Business Professional) and AB-731 (AI Transformation Leader), plus Applied Skills training in AI Chat Workflows and AI Research & Reporting.

The event pages list "Exam Vouchers for eligible participants through the LevelUp program (while supplies last)". Register with your organizational or business email address, because registrations with public-domain addresses (Gmail, Outlook, etc.) are rejected.
