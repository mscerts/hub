:::note
Earn a 50% exam voucher for an exam relevant to the enrolled specialization/professional certificate program.
:::

<Steps>
  1. Enroll to one of the listed courses/specialization/professional certificate programs below.
  2. Complete the enrolled course/specialization/professional certificate and earn the Coursera certificate.
  3. Check the "Messages" menu in the left sidebar inside the program you have completed. You will also be notified through an email if you have not received the voucher immediately after completing the program.
</Steps>

:::caution
Some of the programs below still advertise a 50% discount on an exam that Microsoft has retired. According to Microsoft's [list of retired exams](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/retired-certification-exams?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290), MB-910 retired on December 31, 2025, DP-100 on June 1, 2026, and AI-102 (Microsoft Certified: Azure AI Engineer Associate) on June 30, 2026. The Microsoft Learn page for the [Azure AI Engineer Associate certification](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/azure-ai-engineer/?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290) says that the certification and its renewal assessment are retired. The Coursera pages for the MB-910, DP-100, and AI-102 programs still describe the discount for those exams, so check on Coursera which exam your voucher applies to before you enroll.
:::
