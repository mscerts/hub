## Audience profile

As a candidate for this Microsoft Certification, you have hands-on expertise in implementing and managing end-to-end supply chain processes in Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management and AI-enabled capabilities. You translate customer requirements into practical configurations that align to the business process catalog for Dynamics 365 apps and services.

You deliver effective solutions that meet or exceed the needs of your customers by partnering with solution architects, developers, consultants, business users, project managers, and other stakeholders.

### In this role, you also

- Analyze business requirements across multiple areas.
- Map requirements to system capabilities and design reliable, scalable solutions.
- Identify and manage dependencies between processes.
- Apply industry-recommended practices to implementations.
