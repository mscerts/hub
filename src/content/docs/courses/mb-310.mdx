:::note
This course is a collection of Microsoft Learn modules and documentation pages, arranged by the skills measured in the [official MB-310 study guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/mb-310?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290) (July 25, 2025 version). Each task of the guide links to the Microsoft Learn training and the documentation that cover it. Each skill group opens with its fundamentals, the theory behind its tasks, to read before the tasks. Exercises, guided projects, tutorials and quickstarts are on the [MB-310 labs page](/certs/labs/dynamics/mb-310/), not here.

The course follows the approach of [How to Prepare for Role-Based Exams](/certs/prepare/role-based/): the study guide is the source of truth for what the exam covers, so work through it task by task, with the training to learn a topic and the documentation for the details and for hands-on work. Videos, practice tests and the other resources for the exam are on the [MB-310 Study Materials](/certs/dynamics/mb-310/) page.
:::

## Audience profile

### As a candidate for this exam, you analyze business requirements and translate them into fully realized business processes and solutions that implement industry-recommended practices. You have a fundamental understanding of accounting principles and financial operations as they relate to

- Core tasks in the finance and operations apps
- Manufacturing
- Retail
- Supply chain management operations

### As a candidate for this exam, you specialize in one or more feature sets of Dynamics 365 Finance including

- General ledger
- Accounts payable
- Accounts receivable
- Credit and collections
- Cash and bank management
- Budgeting
- Fixed assets
- Asset leasing
- Tax
- Expense management
- Subscription billing
- Cost accounting
- Cost management
- Consolidation and elimination
