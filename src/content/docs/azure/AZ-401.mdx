:::note
The beta period began on October 14, 2026. Microsoft's [announcement](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/skills-hub-blog/new-microsoft-certified-agentic-devops-engineer-expert/4528356?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290) expects general availability in December. Microsoft also usually releases a beta exam as generally available [10-12 weeks after the beta period begins](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/about-beta-exams?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290), so counting from October 14, 2026 this exam is expected to leave beta between December 23, 2026 and January 6, 2027. Beta scores are released about 10 days after that, so expect results between January 2, 2027 and January 16, 2027. These dates are estimates and can shift, and waiting for a score can take up to 14 weeks. See the [Beta Exams](/certs/vouchers/betaexams/) page for more information.
:::
