:::caution
Exam and its certification, Microsoft Certified: Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management Functional Consultant Associate, are being retired on February 1, 2027.

It will be replaced by [AB-330: Implementing AI-Powered Supply Chain Operations with Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management](/certs/aibusiness/ab-330/), which is in beta now and leads to the new Microsoft Certified: Supply Chain Management AI Consultant Associate certification.

Microsoft announced the retirement together with the new exam: [read the announcement on the Skills Hub blog](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/skills-hub-blog/new-microsoft-certified-supply-chain-management-ai-consultant-associate/4564533?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290).

Microsoft Learn always takes a while to update with certification retirements so don't panic if you can't see it there yet.
:::
