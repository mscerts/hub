:::note
The beta period began on October 14, 2026. Microsoft's [announcement](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/skills-hub-blog/new-github-certified-agentic-ai-business-professional/4564532?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290) expects general availability in December 2026. Microsoft also usually releases a beta exam as generally available [10-12 weeks after the beta period begins](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/about-beta-exams?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290), so counting from October 14, 2026 this exam is expected to leave beta between December 23, 2026 and January 6, 2027. Beta scores are usually released about 10 days after that, which would be between January 2, 2027 and January 16, 2027. However, Microsoft Learn's [certification page](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/agentic-ai-business-professional/?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290) says that scores will be released approximately eight weeks after the beta period concludes, which would be between February 17, 2027 and March 3, 2027. These dates are estimates and can shift, and waiting for a score can take up to 14 weeks. See the [Beta Exams](/certs/vouchers/betaexams/) page for more information.
:::
