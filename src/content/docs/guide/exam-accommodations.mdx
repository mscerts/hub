:::caution
Request your accommodation and wait for approval **before scheduling your exam**, whether you plan to test online or at a test center.
:::

## What can you request?

Examples include extra exam time, assistive technology, a reader or scribe, and a personal care assistant. If you plan to test online and cannot meet the standard room setup or exam-area scan requirements, ask about an adjustment before booking. Available options can differ between online exams and test centers.

## How to request an accommodation

<Steps>

1. Review [Microsoft's request instructions](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/request-accommodations?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290) and check whether you need supporting documents.
2. Register or sign in to the [Pearson VUE Reasonable Adjustments Request System](https://microsoft.onhgcloud.com/). Submit your request and upload any required documents.
3. Wait for the decision email. The email confirming receipt is not an approval.
4. If approved, use the scheduling instructions in the approval email. Keep the email and use the same booking route if you need to reschedule.

</Steps>

:::note
Microsoft says review can take up to **10 business days**. Allow up to **20 days** for the full process, including scheduling.
:::

## What documentation do you need?

Microsoft requires verification for extra time or when another adult needs to be present in the testing area. **You do not need a formal diagnosis.** Examples include a record of past accommodations at school or work, an IEP or 504 plan, or a letter from a teacher or manager. If you do not have the listed documents, use the support contact on Microsoft's documentation page to ask what else you can provide.

## If your request is denied

Open your original request in the Pearson VUE system and select **Request an Appeal** at the bottom of Step 6. Complete and upload the appeal form, add any supporting documents, and submit it. If the decision letter identifies missing information, address that in your appeal.

## When to apply again

According to [Microsoft's accommodations FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/accommodations-faqs?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290), approvals for permanent disabilities remain valid indefinitely unless you want to change your accommodations. For a temporary disability, resubmit after one year. An accommodation for training does not automatically carry over to an exam.

## Before exam day

Try the [Microsoft exam sandbox](https://mscertdemo.starttest.com/) to get familiar with the interface and keyboard navigation. If you need extra time because the exam is not available in your preferred language, see the separate process in [Scheduling an Exam](/certs/guide/schedulingexam/).
