## Associating your certification profile with your organization

Sign into your organizations [Partner Center Account](https://partner.microsoft.com/pc/users/myaccount) (yes, it is very slow to load, it isn't a problem on your side) and under **Your learning** choose the **Associate Microsoft Learning account.**

## [New Microsoft Partner designations](https://learn.microsoft.com/partner-center/introduction-to-pcs?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290)

You can find all the related information in the linked documentation.

The biggest change from the now ending competencies is that requirements require fewer certifications from single individuals and in benefits the Visual Studio Enterprise subscription will no longer have the monthly credits (it will be bulk credits for one subscription instead).

You need 70/100 points to achieve a Microsoft Partner designation. The areas are divided into different performance tiers and skilling. Below are the skilling requirements and how many total points can you gain from certifications.

## [Microsoft Techcommunity for Partners](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/t5/microsoft-partner-community/ct-p/PartnerCommunity?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290)

These are the communication channels, where you can see 99% of any news or changes happening to everything. I recommend subscribing to the topics that interest you to stay up to date on all the cool stuff happening.

## [Microsoft Digital Experiences (CDX/MDX)](https://cdx.transform.microsoft.com)

If you work for a Microsoft Partner, you are eligible to access MDX (if the Partner Organisation set it up). In CDX, you can setup M365 demo tenants, usually filled with content already. These tenants are renewable + you can have multiple active at the same time. There are all sorts of demos and showcases of Microsoft products. Great for any kind of testing or exam preparation. 

  ### [1 Free M365 Tenant](https://cdx.transform.microsoft.com/dashboard?tab=mytenants)

Part of MDX allows you to create 1x 90 day non-renewable M365 tenant. There are multiple types like E5, Business Premium or various specialised tenants. Before creating another one, you need to delete the existing one.

  ### [MDX Experiences](https://cdx.transform.microsoft.com/experiences)

Second part of MDX are Experiences. These have multiple variations, either they are live demonstrations where you use beforementioned tenant to explore/demostrate features live or simulated demonstrations. In simulated demonstrations you get access to a simulated tenant, where you can click around and explore it but it is premade and not a real environment so any updates and changes are not reflected unless it is specifically updated.

The new all in one resource for all Microsoft Partner related events and resources.

  ### [Microsoft Titan Skilling Academy](https://skillupwithlevelup.com/titan)
    Microsoft's new skilling platform for Microsoft Partners. There are 3 areas you can upskill in:
      - Sales Ready
      - Tech Deal Ready (TBD)
      - Project Ready (TBD)

  ### [Microsoft Cloud Champions Program](https://www.cloudchampion.co/link/home)
    Another Microsoft Partner resource. This one is region specific and collects everything happening in your region.

  ### [Microsoft Partner Certification Weeks](/certs/vouchers/partnerweek/)

    Partner Weeks are weeklong events focused on certification related upskilling. They offer:
      - Instructor Lead Training for specific certification exams.
      - Instructor Lead Training labs.
      - Potential free voucher to take the exam.
