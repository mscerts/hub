Not sure which Microsoft certification to take? Answer a few questions, one at a time, and the roadmap points you to the exam or exams that fit. Every exam links to its study materials on this hub.

<CertRoadmap history />

:::note
The roadmap only recommends exams that have a page on this hub and that Microsoft has not retired or announced as retiring. Exams that are in beta are marked as such. Microsoft's own [certification poster](https://aka.ms/TrainCertPoster) lists every certification. See the [Overview](/certs/guide/overview/) for how to read it, and [How to Prepare](/certs/prepare/role-based/) for study guidance.
:::
