:::note
AB-400 is the updated version of exam PL-400: Microsoft Power Platform Developer. This page was the PL-400 page until **October 16, 2026**, and the old PL-400 address now redirects here. On that day registration for PL-400 closed and registration opened exclusively for AB-400, which is available in English. Learners who registered for PL-400 on or before October 16 can schedule and take it through **October 30, 2026**. The certification earned stays Microsoft Certified: Power Platform Developer Associate. Because the exam was updated, check older material against the [AB-400 study guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/ab-400?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290); the resources below that were made for PL-400 carry its code. See [Microsoft's announcement](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/skills-hub-blog/updates-to-azure-cosmos-db-and-power-platform-developer-certifications/4528353?WT.mc_id=studentamb_165290).
:::
